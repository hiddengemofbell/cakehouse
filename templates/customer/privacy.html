Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Liza's Cakehouse, we collect the following information:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (stored securely).
  • Booking information — cake preferences, pickup date and time, budget, payment method, and contact details you provide during checkout.
  • Payment proof — screenshots you upload to confirm downpayment (stored on Cloudinary).
  • Feedback — optional ratings and comments you leave after an order.
  • Google account data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google's OAuth service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Process and manage your cake orders.
  • Communicate with you about your booking status.
  • Allow staff and admins to update your order progress.
  • Improve our service based on feedback.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Payment proof images are hosted on Cloudinary, a trusted cloud image platform. Account and booking data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render.

4. Cookies & Sessions

We use session cookies to keep you logged in while you browse the site. These cookies are essential for the system to work and are not used for tracking or advertising. They expire when you log out or close your browser.

5. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • View the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate details in your account.
  • Delete your account and associated data.

To make any of these requests, contact us at lizascakehouse@yahoo.com.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorized access. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. However, no online system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us at:

  • Email: lizascakehouse@yahoo.com
  • Facebook: Liza's CakeHouse
