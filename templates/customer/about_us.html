Our History

30 years of sweetness in Siniloan

📸 Store photo coming soon

Liza's Cake House was established in 1994 in Siniloan. It started as a small home-based baking business created by a passionate baker who loved making cakes for family gatherings and local celebrations.

Because many people loved the taste and design of the cakes, the business grew through word-of-mouth and recommendations from satisfied customers — no advertising needed.

As the years passed, the shop expanded its menu and began accepting customized cake orders for weddings, debuts, birthdays, and other special events.
