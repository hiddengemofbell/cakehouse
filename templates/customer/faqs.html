Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from Liza's Cakehouse.

Ordering

How do I place a custom cake order?

Create an account or log in, then go to Book a Cake. Fill in your preferred pickup date, flavor, size, design theme, and any special requests. Once submitted, our team will review and confirm your booking.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend placing your order at least 5–7 days before your pickup date for standard designs, and at least 2 weeks for complex or multi-tiered cakes.

Can I order for same-day pickup?

Same-day orders are subject to availability. Please message us directly on Facebook to check before placing a same-day booking.

What flavors and sizes are available?

We offer a variety of flavors including chocolate, vanilla, ube, mocha, and more. Sizes range from 6-inch to 16-inch and multi-tiered options. Let us know your preference when booking and we'll confirm availability.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash and Bank Transfer. Cash on pickup is also accepted for some orders — confirm this with us when your booking is accepted.

Is a downpayment required?

Yes. A downpayment is required to confirm your order. You can upload your payment screenshot directly in the booking form. The remaining balance is settled upon pickup.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancellations must be made as early as possible. Downpayments may be non-refundable depending on how close to the pickup date the cancellation is made. Contact us directly to discuss.

Pickup & Delivery

Do you offer delivery?

Currently, we operate as a pickup-only shop. You can pick up your cake at 51 M Acevida, Siniloan, Laguna between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM.

What are your pickup hours?

Pickup is available daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Please select your preferred pickup time during booking.

What if I can't pick up on my scheduled date?

Please contact us as soon as possible to reschedule. We'll do our best to accommodate changes, but this depends on our current schedule and cake storage limitations.

Online System

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. You need to create an account to place and track bookings. You can sign up with your email or continue with Google.

How do I track my order status?

Log in and go to My Bookings. You'll see the current booking status (Pending, Accepted, Declined) and cake production status (Not Started, Ongoing, Completed) for each order.

Can I leave feedback after my order?

Yes! Once your cake has been marked as completed, you can rate your experience and leave a comment from the booking detail page.

Still have questions? Contact us and we'll get back to you.
